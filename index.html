Howling Whispers

Worlds imagined. Characters remembered. Stories set in motion.

Howling Whispers is a connected ecosystem for building, preserving, exploring, and simulating fictional worlds and characters through worldbuilding, interactive fiction, AI simulation, and roleplay.

Explore Orbis or join the Howling Whispers Discord.

The ecosystem

Orbis: The Library of Howling Whispers

Orbis stores worlds, characters, places, factions, items, species, societies, families, memories, and related canon. It includes NovelAI integration for supported AI-assisted generation workflows.

Speculus: Simulation

Speculus is a roleplay and character/world simulation environment connected to Orbis, with NovelAI among its supported AI-generation capabilities. View the public Speculus roadmap.

Fabula: World Runtime

Fabula is the in-development runtime layer for persistent playable worlds, including travel, inventory, economy, encounters, consequences, and character and world state.

Mouseion: Creation & Research

Mouseion develops and analyzes canonical content, systems, worlds, characters, and future simulation intelligence.

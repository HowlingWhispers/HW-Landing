Howling Whispers

Every whisper becomes a world.

An independent home for heavily experimental AI roleplay, worldbuilding, and new ideas. Features may change, break, or be rebuilt.

Enter Orbis or join the Howling Whispers Discord.

The core ecosystem

Orbis: Canon & Library

The canonical library and worldbuilding platform. Orbis stores worlds, characters, places, items, factions, species, societies, families, memories, and connected canon.

Speculus: Simulation

A roleplay and simulation environment that loads Orbis canon to test characters, scenes, relationships, context, and behavior.

Fabula: Persistent World Runtime

In development: the planned runtime layer for long-running worlds with travel, time, inventory, economy, encounters, dice, consequences, and persistent state.

Studium: Research & Analysis

In development: the research system designed to study simulation and runtime history, identifies patterns and gaps, and can prepare new canon proposals for review.
